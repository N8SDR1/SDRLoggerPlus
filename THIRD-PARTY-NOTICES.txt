Origin of the codebase
-----------------------
SDRLoggerPlus originates from Log4YM by Brian Keating (EI6LF), released into the
public domain under the Unlicense (https://unlicense.org). Several feature
designs were re-implemented based on SDRLogger+
(awards tracking, weather alerts, S.A.T. integration, Hot List, scheduled
backups); no SDRLogger+ source code is included.
